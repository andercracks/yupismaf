📶 Internet Speed Guide

Learn what your internet speed means and how to improve your connection.

📊 Understanding Your Results

🚀 Download Speed

Measures how quickly data is downloaded from the internet. Higher download speeds provide smoother browsing, streaming and file downloads.

📤 Upload Speed

Upload speed determines how fast you send data online. It is important for video calls, cloud backups and live streaming.

⚡ Ping (Latency)

Ping measures response time. Lower latency provides a better experience in gaming and video conferences.

💻 Recommended Internet Speed

Activity Recommended Speed
Web Browsing 5 Mbps

4K Streaming 35 Mbps

Online Gaming 25 Mbps
Large Downloads 100+ Mbps

⭐ Speed Rating

🟢 Excellent

Perfect for gaming, 4K streaming and many connected devices.

🔵 Good

Great for everyday browsing, meetings and entertainment.

🟡 Average

Suitable for basic use, but heavy activities may be slower.

🔴 Slow

You may experience buffering, lag and slow downloads.

🛠 Tips to Improve Your Internet

  • Restart your router regularly.
  • Place your router in an open location.
  • Use Ethernet for the best performance.
  • Use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band when possible.
  • Avoid heavy downloads while gaming.
  • Keep your router firmware updated.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mbps?

Mbps stands for Megabits per second and measures internet speed.

What is Ping?

Ping measures the response time between your device and a server.

Why is my speed lower than my plan?

Wi-Fi interference, network congestion and your device can affect results.

How often should I test my speed?

Testing once every few weeks helps monitor your connection.
